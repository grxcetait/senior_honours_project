%The performance of the PCA foreground removal method was evaluated by comparing the recovered residual signal to the HI signal. $N_{FG} = 3$ principal components were removed and then the cylindrical power spectrum was plotted. This is seen in \Cref{fig:cylindrical_power_spectrum_hi_signal}. 

From observing the ratio plot on the right panel, minimum thresholds of $k_\perp = 0.025$ and $k_\parallel = 0.0$ were defined for the foreground cleaning to minimise HI signal loss. 

The resulting 1D power spectrum from the foreground cleaning having implemented the minimum threshold can be seen in \Cref{fig:log_power_spectrum_hi_signal}.
